Is frequent sleepwalking a sign of an underlying medical condition?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Frequent sleepwalking may signal an underlying medical issue. Sleepwalking is classified as a parasomnia, a group of sleep disorders involving abnormal behaviors during sleep. Possible causes include sleep apnea, restless leg syndrome, fever, stress, certain medications, or neurological conditions.
