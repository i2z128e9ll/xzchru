Why Does Wegovy Cause Constipation?

Wegovy belongs to a class called GLP-1 receptor agonists. By mimicking the hormone glucagon-like peptide-1, it:

Slows stomach emptying: Food stays in your stomach longer, which helps you feel full but also reduces the urge to have a bowel movement.

Alters gut motility: It decreases the contractions that move food through your intestines.

Practical Tips to Manage Wegovy Constipation

Before considering a medication change, try lifestyle and over-the-counter strategies:

1. Hydration

Aim for at least 8–10 cups of water daily.

Include hydrating foods (e.g., watermelon, cucumbers).

2. Dietary Fiber

Soluble fiber: Oats, apples, beans

Insoluble fiber: Whole grains, nuts, vegetables

Add fiber slowly to avoid gas and bloating.

3. Physical Activity

Brisk walking (20–30 minutes daily)

Yoga poses that massage the intestines (e.g., "wind-relieving" pose)

Light strength training

4. Bowel Routine

Establish a consistent bathroom schedule, ideally after meals.

Give yourself 10–15 minutes without distractions.

5. Over-the-Counter Aids

Stool softeners (e.g., docusate sodium) make stool easier to pass.

Osmotic laxatives (e.g., polyethylene glycol) draw water into the bowel.

Stimulant laxatives (e.g., senna, bisacodyl) trigger bowel contractions (use short-term).

Always read labels and follow dosing instructions. If you're uncertain which product to choose, speak with a pharmacist or your doctor.

When to Consider Switching Medications

If constipation persists despite these measures, discuss alternatives with your healthcare provider. Considerations include:

Severity and impact• Is constipation affecting your quality of life?• Are you at risk for complications (e.g., hemorrhoids, fissures)?

Dose adjustments• Sometimes reducing the dose by one injection level helps.

Alternative GLP-1 agonists• Different drugs in this class may affect individuals differently.

Non-GLP-1 options• Medications like bupropion/naltrexone or orlistat work via other mechanisms.

Non-drug approaches• Intensify lifestyle interventions before stopping a medication that's otherwise effective.

Changing weight-loss medications can impact your results and may require re-titration. Never alter doses or stop treatment without professional guidance.
