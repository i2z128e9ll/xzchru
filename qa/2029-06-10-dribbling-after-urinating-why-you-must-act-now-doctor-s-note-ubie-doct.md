Explanation

Dribbling after urinating, also known as post-micturition dribble, can be a common issue for some people. This happens when a small amount of urine leaks out after you think you are done urinating. One reason for this can be weak pelvic floor muscles. These muscles help control the flow of urine, and if they are not strong enough, they might not be able to stop the flow completely.

According to research, pelvic floor exercises can help strengthen these muscles and reduce dribbling. These exercises involve tightening and relaxing the muscles that you use to stop urinating.

Another study found that a medication called tadalafil, which is often used for other urinary problems, might also help reduce dribbling in men. This medication can improve muscle function and help control urine flow better.

If you experience this issue, doing pelvic floor exercises regularly or talking to a healthcare provider about possible treatments might be helpful. You can also use this free symptom checker to better understand your urinary symptoms and get personalized guidance on next steps.
