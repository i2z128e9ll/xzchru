Swelling near the elbow can be treated with these steps:

Rest: Avoid activities that strain or irritate the elbow. Elevate: Keep your arm raised above heart level to reduce swelling. Cold compress: Apply ice packs for 15–20 minutes several times a day to reduce inflammation and pain. Medication: Over-the-counter anti-inflammatory drugs like ibuprofen can help ease pain and swelling. Protect the area: Use a brace or support if recommended by a healthcare provider. Monitor symptoms: Watch for worsening pain, redness, warmth, or fever, which may indicate infection or other complications.
