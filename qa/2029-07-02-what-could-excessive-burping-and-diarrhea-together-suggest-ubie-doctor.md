Explanation

Excessive burping and diarrhea occuring together may be linked to a few different health issues. One possibility is small intestinal fungal overgrowth. This condition occurs when there is too much fungus in the small intestine, which can lead to symptoms like gas and diarrhea.

Another potential cause is related to the thyroid gland. The thyroid helps control how your body uses energy, and if it is not working right, it can affect your gut and cause symptoms like diarrhea.

Food intolerance is another distinct possibility. Sorbitol intolerance is an example. Sorbitol is a type of sugar found in some foods. If your body has trouble digesting it, you might experience gas and diarrhea.

If you notice these symptoms, it is a good idea to talk to a healthcare provider to find out what might be causing them and how to feel better.
