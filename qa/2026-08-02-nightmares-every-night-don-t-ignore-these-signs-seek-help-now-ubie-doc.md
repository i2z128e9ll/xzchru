Is it normal to have nightmares every night and when should I seek help?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Frequent nightmares every night are not considered normal, particularly when they cause emotional distress, disrupt sleep, or interfere with daily functioning. Nightly nightmares can signal an underlying issue such as stress, anxiety, PTSD, medication side effects, or a sleep disorder like nightmare disorder. If you're experiencing persistent nightmares, it's important to consult a healthcare professional for evaluation and treatment.

Nightmares are bad dreams that can make you feel scared or upset. Having them once in a while is normal, but having them every night might not be. Some people, like those who have been through very hard times, might have nightmares more often. For example, people who have experienced trauma might have nightmares frequently.

[Night Don Ignore](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/blog/2760186777.html)

Nightmares can also be linked to poor sleep quality. If nightmares make it hard for you to sleep or affect your mood during the day, it's important to talk to someone who can help, like a doctor or therapist.

Here are some signs that you should seek help:

Nightmares happen almost every night.

They make you very upset or scared.

They make it hard for you to sleep well.

They affect how you feel or act during the day.
