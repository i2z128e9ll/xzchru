What is Liposomal Vitamin C?

Liposomal Vitamin C is a unique form of Vitamin C that’s designed to be more easily absorbed by your body. What makes it different from other forms is that it uses liposomes—small particles made of fat-like substances that act as a protective barrier around the Vitamin C. These liposomes help shield the Vitamin C from being broken down too quickly in your digestive tract. This protection allows more of the Vitamin C to reach your bloodstream and, ultimately, your cells.

Liposomes work by surrounding the Vitamin C in a way that mimics your body’s own cell membranes. This structure lets them pass through the digestive system with less interference, delivering a higher percentage of the vitamin to where your body needs it the most. For people who find regular Vitamin C less effective or too hard on their stomach, liposomal Vitamin C can be a helpful alternative that allows for a more efficient and gentler delivery.

The Difference Between Liposomal and Regular Vitamin C

There are some significant differences between liposomal Vitamin C and regular Vitamin C (also known as ascorbic acid). The most important difference is in its absorption and bioavailability—which is how well your body can actually use the Vitamin C you take. Regular Vitamin C can be harder for your body to absorb because much of it gets broken down as it navigates through your digestive system. This breakdown can lead to lower absorption rates, which means that your body might not be getting as much of the Vitamin C as you’d expect.
