Leaving a tampon or menstrual cup in too long can cause serious infections, including Toxic Shock Syndrome (TSS), a rare but life-threatening condition. TSS is triggered by bacterial toxins and can escalate quickly, causing fever, rash, low blood pressure, and organ complications if untreated.

If you suspect a forgotten tampon or cup, remove it as soon as possible and monitor for symptoms like fever, dizziness, vomiting, or flu-like feelings. Prompt medical attention is critical if symptoms appear.
