Low back pain often improves with simple, low-impact exercises. Walking boosts circulation and mobility, gentle stretching relieves muscle tension, and core-strengthening moves (like planks, bridges, and pelvic tilts) support the spine to reduce strain. Together, these activities can ease pain, improve flexibility, and help prevent future flare-ups.

That said, low back pain can stem from many causes — muscle strain, disc issues, nerve compression, or something else entirely — and the right exercises depend on the underlying problem.
