Explanation

Several scalp conditions can cause hair loss if they are not managed properly.

Female Androgenetic Alopecia: This is a common condition that can lead to hair thinning and loss. It occurs due to hormonal changes and genetic factors. If left untreated, it can cause significant hair loss over time.

Scarring Alopecia: This condition involves the formation of scar tissue on the scalp, which can destroy hair follicles and lead to permanent hair loss. Early treatment is crucial to prevent further damage.

Dissecting Cellulitis: This is an inflammatory condition that affects the scalp, often after procedures like hair restoration. It can lead to painful lumps and hair loss if not addressed.

Each of these conditions requires timely medical attention to prevent or minimize hair loss. If you notice changes in your scalp or hair, consulting a healthcare provider can guide you to the right treatment options.
