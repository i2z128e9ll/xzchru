While these medications have been a major breakthrough, they’re not intended to be a quick fix.

GLP-1s work best as part of a larger holistic care plan, and people who take them should understand the potential risks and side effects. They also need to be ready to make a long-term commitment to their health. “GLP-1s have changed the landscape for obesity care, and people who take them should know what to expect with these treatments, as being prepared may help with long-term success,” says Kimberly Gudzune, MD, MPH, a board-certified obesity medicine physician and affiliate faculty at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health in Baltimore.

Thinking about adding a GLP-1 to your obesity treatment plan? Take this assessment, developed in collaboration with Dr. Gudzune, to see how much you know about these medications and whether you’re ready to integrate one into your care plan.
