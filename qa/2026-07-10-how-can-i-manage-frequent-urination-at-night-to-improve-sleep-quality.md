Frequent urination at night, also known as nocturia, can disrupt sleep and affect your daily life. Here are some ways to manage it:

Fluid Management: Try reducing your fluid intake in the evening, especially beverages like caffeine and alcohol, which can increase urine production.

Health Conditions: Nocturia can be linked to conditions like diabetes or benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) in men. Managing these conditions with the help of a healthcare provider can reduce nighttime urination.

Consult a Healthcare Provider: It is important to talk to a healthcare provider to understand the underlying causes of nocturia and explore treatment options. They can provide personalized advice based on your health needs.
