What are common reasons for being unable to sleep after surgery and how can it be addressed?

Internal Medicine

Post-surgery sleep problems are common and often caused by pain, stress, anxiety, or disruptions to your normal sleep schedule. Effective solutions include managing pain with your doctor's guidance, creating a calm, comfortable sleep environment, practicing relaxation techniques, and seeking support for emotional well-being during recovery.

Because trouble sleeping after surgery can stem from many overlapping causes—some minor, others requiring medical attention—it's important to understand what's driving your symptoms before deciding on next steps.
