Internal Medicine

Raynaud's phenomenon can cause itchy and sore nipples by restricting blood flow to the area. This condition triggers blood vessels to spasm, often in response to cold or stress, leading to nipple pain, color changes (white, blue, or red), tingling, burning, and itching. While Raynaud's commonly affects fingers and toes, it can also target the nipples, particularly in breastfeeding women.

Symptoms often worsen with cold exposure and improve with warmth. If you're experiencing persistent nipple discomfort, identifying the underlying cause is key to finding relief, as treatments vary widely depending on whether the issue stems from Raynaud's, infection, hormonal changes, or skin conditions.
