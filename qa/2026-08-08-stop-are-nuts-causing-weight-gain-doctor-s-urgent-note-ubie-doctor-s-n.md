Can eating too many nuts cause weight gain? Yes, eating excessive amounts of nuts can contribute to weight gain because nuts are calorie-dense, and consuming more calories than your body burns leads to weight gain. However, research shows that moderate nut consumption (about 1 ounce or a small handful per day) is not linked to weight gain and may actually support weight management, heart health, and better appetite control.
