Explanation

[low testosterone](https://insulin-pathways.hbsnzj.com/blog/3399311162.html)

When someone feels depressed, they often have little energy and feel very tired. Depression affects how our brain works, which can lower our energy and make everyday activities feel harder. Tiredness is one of the common signs that people with depression report. Testosterone is a hormone that helps keep our energy up and our mood steady. When levels of testosterone are low, a person might feel even more tired and may have a lower mood. In simple words, low testosterone can make it harder to feel energetic and happy. Some studies show that lower testosterone levels are linked with more tiredness and mood problems. This means that both depression and low testosterone can work together to cause fatigue and other symptoms like feeling sad or lacking motivation. If you're experiencing persistent tiredness alongside mood changes, consider using a free symptom checker to better understand what might be causing your symptoms. Understanding this connection can help doctors and patients think about different ways to feel better and improve energy levels.
