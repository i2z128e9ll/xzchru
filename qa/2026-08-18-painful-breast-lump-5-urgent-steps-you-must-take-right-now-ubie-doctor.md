Explanation

[Steps You](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

Finding a painful lump in your breast can be concerning, and it is important to have it checked by a doctor. Painful breast lumps can be caused by benign breast conditions, which are non-cancerous and quite common. These might include things like cysts or fibrocystic changes that can cause discomfort and lumps.

However, while less common, breast cancer can also present as a painful lump. According to the medical literature, localized breast pain can sometimes be a symptom of breast cancer, so it is crucial to have any new or unusual breast changes evaluated by a healthcare professional.

Steps to Take:

Schedule a Doctor's Visit: Make an appointment with your healthcare provider to discuss the lump and any other symptoms you might have.

Medical Evaluation: Your doctor may perform a physical exam and possibly recommend imaging tests like a mammogram or ultrasound to understand the nature of the lump.

Follow-Up: Based on the findings, your doctor will advise you on the next steps, which might include monitoring the lump or further testing.
