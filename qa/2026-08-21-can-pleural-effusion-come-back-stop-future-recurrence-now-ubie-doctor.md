Explanation

Pleural effusion is when fluid builds up around the lungs. It can happen again after treatment. There are ways to try and stop it from coming back.

Prevention Methods:

Pleurodesis: This is a treatment where medicine is put into the space around the lungs to make them stick together. This helps stop fluid from building up again.

Surgical Options: Sometimes, doctors might do surgery to remove part of the lining around the lungs. This can help prevent the fluid from coming back.

Other Invasive Methods: Doctors might use other special tools or methods to help keep the fluid from returning.

Each method has its own risks and benefits, and the best choice depends on the person's health and situation.
