Individuals who are suffering from arthritis and joint pain should consult with a medical professional so that they can get help with managing the pain and symptoms. To obtain a proper diagnosis, a physical exam is needed.

The medical practitioner will check the range of motion in your joints and different areas for swelling and tenderness. The doctor will also evaluate your overall health to determine if there are underlying conditions that could cause your symptoms.

An imaging exam or scan can also help your doctor get a clear picture of joints, bones, and soft tissues. This can be done either by MRI, ultrasound, or X-ray.

The imaging scans can determine bone fractures or dislocations that might cause pain. Breakdown of cartilage around your joints will also show up in the scans, as well as soft tissue inflammation.
