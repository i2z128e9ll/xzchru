What Is Maca Root?

Maca (Lepidium meyenii) is a Peruvian plant in the cruciferous family. Traditionally, Andean people have used maca to:

Enhance stamina and endurance

Support hormonal balance

Improve sexual desire

Key compounds in maca include macamides, macaenes, and glucosinolates. These may act on the hypothalamus and pituitary to influence sex hormones.

Scientific Evidence for Maca and Libido

• A 2002 randomized trial (Gonzales et al.) found that 1.5–3 g/day of maca for 12 weeks improved self-reported libido in healthy men.• A 2015 meta-analysis (Shin et al.) of four placebo-controlled trials concluded that maca has a significant positive effect on sexual desire in both men and women.• Doses in studies range from 1.5 to 3 g daily of gelatinized maca powder.

What Is Creatine?

Creatine is a naturally occurring compound found in muscles and the brain. It helps regenerate ATP, the body's main energy currency, during high-intensity exercise. It's one of the most studied sports supplements.

How Creatine May Affect Libido

While creatine is best known for physical performance, a few mechanisms might influence sexual health:

Enhanced exercise capacity can improve body image and confidence, indirectly boosting drive.

One study (van der Merwe et al., 2009) reported a 56% rise in dihydrotestosterone (DHT) after five weeks of creatine loading (20 g/day for 7 days, then 5 g/day). DHT is a potent androgen linked to sexual function.

Better overall energy and recovery may reduce fatigue, a common libido killer.

Dosage and Timing

• Loading Phase (optional): 20 g/day split into 4 doses for 5–7 days• Maintenance Phase: 3–5 g/day thereafter

Take with plenty of water and a carbohydrate-rich meal to improve absorption.

Safety and Side Effects

Decades of research support safety in healthy adults

No major hormonal disruptions reported at normal doses

Some users experience bloating or weight gain (water retention)

Rare reports of digestive upset

Safety First: When to Seek Professional Help

Natural supplements can help mild-to-moderate libido issues, but they're not a cure for serious conditions. If you experience any of the following, please seek medical attention right away:

Sudden, severe drop in sexual desire

Pain during sex or erections

Symptoms of heart trouble (chest pain, shortness of breath)

Unexplained weight loss or fatigue
