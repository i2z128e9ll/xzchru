Can Cinnamon Lower Blood Sugar?

Yes, cinnamon may help lower blood sugar levels, particularly in people with type 2 diabetes. Research suggests it can improve insulin sensitivity and reduce fasting blood glucose. However, the ideal dosage is not clearly established, and effects vary by cinnamon type (Ceylon vs. Cassia) and individual health factors.
