The Lymphatic System

The lymphatic system acts as the body’s detox network, transporting lymph, a fluid that carries immune cells and removes waste. Unlike the circulatory system, which relies on the heart to pump blood, the lymphatic system depends on movement and physical activity to keep fluids flowing.

Key functions of the lymphatic system include:

Detoxification: Clearing waste and toxins.

Immunity: Transporting white blood cells to fight infection.

Fluid balance: Preventing swelling and regulating hydration.

Inflammation control: Supporting the body’s response to injury and stress.

When the lymphatic system becomes overwhelmed due to inactivity, stress, or inflammation, it can lead to symptoms such as bloating, puffiness, and sluggishness.
