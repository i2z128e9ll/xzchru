What is Mastopathy?

A condition where a painful lump forms in the breast due to imbalances in female hormone levels, causing symptoms to occur before and during menstruation.

Typical Symptoms of Mastopathy

Breast lumps

Inappropriate milk production e.g. not pregnant or with recent childbirth, or as a male

Left-sided chest pain

Pain when pressing on the breast

Difference in nipple heights or appearance of the breast/s such as a dimpled area

Smooth, round and movable lump under the skin

Right sided chest pain

Chest pain

Diagnostic Questions for Mastopathy

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you noticed any lumps in your breasts?

Are you producing breast milk without being pregnant or giving birth, or if you are a man?

Do you have pain on the left side of your chest?

Does pressing on your breast cause pain?

Have you noticed any differences in your nipple heights or breast appearance?

Treatment of Mastopathy

This condition may improve without treatment. Meanwhile, medications and hormone treatment can be given to alleviate pain. Sometimes, it is caused by diabetes, and maintaining good blood sugar levels may help prevent its recurrence.
