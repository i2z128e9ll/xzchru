Full Bio

Chanel Ferrante, MSN, APRN, AGNP-BC, is a board-certified Adult-Gerontology Nurse Practitioner with experience in internal medicine, urgent care, concierge medicine, functional wellness, hormone replacement, and aesthetics. She blends conventional and integrative approaches to help patients address current concerns while building a strong foundation for long-term health. Chanel earned her Master of Science in Nursing from UCLA, with training in Adult-Gerontology and environmental health. Her clinical background began in the operating room and later expanded into urgent care and concierge medicine in Beverly Hills and Malibu. She has completed additional training in functional medicine, hormone replacement, natural medicine, and aesthetic care. Chanel has also participated in medical outreach through Mission of Hope in Haiti and supported community outreach efforts in Maui following the Lahaina fires. She is committed to providing attentive, individualized care that considers the full picture of each patient’s health, lifestyle, and goals.
