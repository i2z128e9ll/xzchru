Why Shilajit Is Important for Preventing DNA Damage

Shilajit is a natural, sticky resin found primarily in the Himalayas and certain mountain ranges around the world. For centuries, traditional medicine systems have credited Shilajit with rejuvenating properties. Modern research is now uncovering how Shilajit supports DNA integrity and overall cellular health—key factors in healthy aging, energy production, and disease prevention.

What Is Shilajit?

A mineral-rich substance formed by the slow decomposition of plant matter over centuries

Contains fulvic acid, humic acid, trace minerals, amino acids, and antioxidants

Traditionally used in Ayurveda for boosting vitality, strength, and resilience

Understanding DNA Damage and Cellular Integrity

Our cells rely on DNA to store and transmit genetic information. Everyday factors—environmental toxins, UV exposure, poor diet, stress—can cause DNA damage. If not repaired, this damage can lead to:

Mutations

Cellular malfunction

Accelerated aging

Increased risk of chronic diseases

Maintaining cellular integrity means preserving DNA structure, ensuring efficient repair, and promoting healthy cell division.

Suggested Usage and Safety

Form: Standardized Shilajit resin or purified powder

Dosage: 200–500 mg per day, ideally in two divided doses with meals

Duration: At least 8–12 weeks to observe significant changes in energy and biomarkers

Safety considerations:

Choose a reputable source to avoid contaminants (heavy metals, additives)

Start with a low dose (100 mg) to assess tolerance

Possible mild side effects: digestive upset, headache—often resolved by adjusting dose

Contraindications: pregnancy, breastfeeding, certain autoimmune conditions; speak to your doctor if unsure

Practical Tips

Mix Shilajit powder in warm water, milk, or herbal tea

Combine with a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and lean protein

Avoid excessive alcohol and smoking, which increase oxidative stress

Stay hydrated and manage stress through meditation, yoga, or gentle exercise
