What to Consider

Fabrics

Merino Wool

It's highly breathable, moisture-wicking, odor-resistant, and naturally insulating. Ideal for both mild and cold weather. It also feels softer than traditional wool, making it a comfortable option for those with sensitive skin.

Blended Fabrics (e.g., Merino Wool/Polyester or Cotton/Spandex blends)

These blends combine the benefits of different materials for improved stretch, moisture management, and durability. For example, spandex can enhance flexibility, while a wool blend adds warmth without bulk.

Thermal Knits (Waffle Knit or Ribbed Textures)

These fabrics are designed with textured patterns that trap air, providing better insulation while being breathable. Waffle-knit thermals are often soft and comfortable for layering.

Cotton

Soft and comfortable against the skin. Cotton is a good option for mild temperatures or lounging. Not great for moisture management; it absorbs sweat and retains moisture, which can make you feel cold once you sweat or in wet conditions.

Nylon

Durable, lightweight, and moisture-wicking. Nylon-based thermals are often used for activewear and outdoor activities due to their strength and water-resistant properties.

Fit

Slim Fit

Offers a close-to-the-body, sleek silhouette. Ideal for layering under your outerwear styles without adding bulk. It provides a more modern look and works well for active or performance-based activities.

Regular Fit

A balanced fit that provides comfort without being too tight or too loose. This fit is versatile for everyday wear and layering. May not be as streamlined as a slim fit for those looking for a more tailored look.

Relaxed Fit

A regular fit gives more room in the chest, waist, and arms, providing maximum comfort, especially for lounging or for those who prefer a more relaxed, casual style. Ideal for those who need more freedom of movement.

Compression Fit

Designed to fit snugly to the body, providing muscle support and improving circulation, which can be helpful for athletic performance. These are often used for performance or recovery. They also work great for outdoor activities such as camping and hiking.
