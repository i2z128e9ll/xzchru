# Ugg Just Dropped a New Men's Clog, and It's Surprisingly Good

Ugg just dropped a brand-new men's clog, the versatile Otzo.

The clog represents a rare men's-only launch from one of our favorite loungewear brands.

The Otzo is a durable and leather-constructed alternative to the classic Tasman slippers.

UGG SLIPPERS ARE a cultural mainstay, and they’ve taken on something of a new life in recent years. You’ll find people wearing them at the grocery store, out to eat, and, at least in Gen Z’s case, in the gym.

And while we do love wearing our Uggs at all times, it's not always the most sanitary of practices, especially if you’re using slippers as, you know, actual slippers. But Ugg just solved the problem for us with one of its biggest men’s drops in recent memory.

Enter the Otzo Clog, the brand’s first leather men’s clog. It’s a bit of a departure from Ugg’s typical sheepskin-lined slippers, such as the classic Tasman II. The Otzo, though, still manages to capture the Tasman’s classic silhouette—but with some notable differences.

Ugg Men's Otzo Clog

The Otzo offers a sleeker, more subdued approach, and features a nutback leather upper that feels more distinguished and a little less busy than the classic slipper. The Otzo comes in three distinct colorways: Black, Dark Chestnut, and Burnt Olive. It's also, obviously, much more durable, thanks to its waterproof leather upper, lightweight rubber outsole, and contoured EVA footbed for all-day comfort. The EVA footbed also offers more support and a more stable movement than the traditional slipper.

The leather and wool lining is another simple yet effective touch, and gives the clogs some extra breathability. Some clogs can get sweaty fast, but that doesn't seem to be the case here. Ugg offers another line of protection, though: the lining is infused with a subtle peppermint-based anti-odor treatment. An anti-odor treatment was similarly applied ot the leather and microfiber sockliner cover, which we can appreciate. This also allows the Otzo to withstand the test of time better.

Of course, this appears to be the overarching point of the Otzo—keeping your slippers in-house. Now you can own a pair of Ugg slip-ons that offer both support and comfort—without sacrificing either. We still probably wouldn't recommend wearing them as gym shoes, though, but we won't judge. They're that comfortable.
