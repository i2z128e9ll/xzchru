# Watch This Pro Climber Take on the U.S. Navy SEALs Physical Fitness Test

Earlier this month, professional climber Magnus Midtbø took on a version of the U.S. Marine Corps Physical Fitness Test, which he passed with flying colors, earning an impressive 280 out of a max of 300.

For his latest YouTube video, he's taking on another U.S. military branch's fitness test: the U.S. Navy SEALs Physical Fitness Test. It's a notoriously grueling challenge that fitness personalities before him, like the Buff Dudes, Ethan Payne, and MattDoesFitness and Mike Thurston, have all attempted.

The U.S. Navy SEALs Fitness Test consists of:

2 minutes pushups

2 minutes situps

As many pullups as possible

1.5 mile run

[This Pro](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/)

500 yard swim

First up for Midtbø is the 500 yard swim, which he needs to complete in a minimum time of 12:30 minutes, or a competitive time of 8 minutes.

He easily passes, clocking in at 10:30.

"We were considering doing this in the ocean. That would have been a terrible idea," says a winded Midtbø.

[Pro Climber](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/)

[SEALs Physical Fitness](https://metabolic-horizon.bbsxyxy.com/blog/8535857532.html)

Next up is situps, with a minimum of 50 reps and a competitive number of 80 to 100 reps. Midtbø grinds out 103 reps, flying by the standard mark.

[Physical Fitness](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/)

"It was good," he says as he quickly preps for the next test, the pushups. The discipline has the same standards as the situps, with a minimum of 50 reps and a competitive number of 80 to 100 reps. He taps out at 90. "Ugh, so hard," says Midtbø. "The first minute is so easy. I don't know if it would be smart to pace yourself."

Next up: pullups to failure.

"This is going to be a real battle for me," says Midtbø. "I'm pretty sore. I also use chalk, and you're not allowed to use chalk. So that may be my cryptonite."

He gets to it, and knocks out 34 reps.

"I thought I would be able to do more," says Midtbø. The competitive standards top out around 20 reps, so it's safe to say that he performed well.

Afterwards, they head to a track for the 1.5 mile run, which he needs to complete in a minimum time of 10:30 minutes, or a competitive time of 9 to 10 minutes.

[Climber Take](https://leanorbit.dns0663.com/blog/5052612053.html)

"I'm aiming for 9 minutes," says Midtbø.

He fuels up with some sips of Coca-Cola, saying, "I don't normally drink Coca-Cola, but today (I do) because I need that extra sugar."

He crushes the run in 8:22.

His calculates his final score to 735, which he read would "meet or exceeded the minimum scores necessary to qualify for a SEAL contract." But that wouldn't guarantee someone a place with the elite unit. "You MAY NOT be competitive enough," he reads. "Keep working hard to improve."
