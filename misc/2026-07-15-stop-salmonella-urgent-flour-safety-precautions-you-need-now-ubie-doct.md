# Stop Salmonella: Urgent Flour Safety Precautions You Need Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/15/2026

What precautions should be taken to prevent salmonella contamination in flour?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Salmonella in flour can be prevented by cooking flour thoroughly to at least 160°F (71°C), which kills harmful bacteria, and by storing flour in a sealed container in a cool, dry place. Avoid tasting raw dough or batter, wash hands and utensils after handling raw flour, and keep it away from ready-to-eat foods to prevent cross-contamination.

If you've eaten raw flour or suspect Salmonella exposure, symptoms like diarrhea, fever, stomach cramps, and nausea can appear within 6 hours to 6 days.
