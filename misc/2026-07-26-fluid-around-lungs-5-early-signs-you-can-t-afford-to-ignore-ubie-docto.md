Internal Medicine

Early signs of pleural effusion (fluid around the lungs) commonly include shortness of breath, sharp chest pain that worsens when breathing deeply, and a persistent dry cough. Additional symptoms may include fever, fatigue, or difficulty breathing when lying flat. Because pleural effusion can signal underlying conditions such as heart failure, pneumonia, or other serious illnesses, early recognition is critical.

Explanation

Fluid around the lungs is known as pleural effusion. When this happens, you might feel like it's hard to breathe or that your chest hurts. You might also start coughing. These are signs that you should talk to a doctor.

What to Look For

Difficulty Breathing: This might feel like you can't catch your breath.

Chest Pain: The pain can be sharp and might get worse when you breathe in.
