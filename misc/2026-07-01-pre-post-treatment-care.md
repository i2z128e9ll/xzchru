CO2 Laser Pre-Treatment Instructions

Adhering to these instructions is critical for safety, optimal healing, and best results.Failure to follow pre-treatment guidelines may result in treatment postponement.

LIGHT TREATMENT

2 Weeks Before Treatment

Avoid sun exposure, tanning beds, and self-tanners

Use broad-spectrum SPF 30–50 daily

Discontinue retinoids (retinol, tretinoin, adapalene)

Apply Alastin Regenerating Skin Nectar (thin layer). This helps strengthen the skin barrier and reduce inflammation from laser treatment.

[Post Treatment Care](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/blog/1276897803.html)

Avoid waxing, threading, or depilatory creams in the treatment area (shaving is permitted)

1 Week Before Treatment

Discontinue exfoliating acids (glycolic, salicylic, lactic), scrubs, and harsh active skincare

Notify your provider if you are currently taking antibiotics or have any active infection, as treatment timing or post-procedure care may need to be adjusted

Notify your provider of any new skin irritation, rash, or break in the skin

[Treatment Care](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

24–48 Hours Before Treatment

Avoid strenuous exercise

Do not apply makeup, lotions, sunscreen, or active skincare products to the treatment area

Day of Treatment

Arrive with clean skin — no makeup, sunscreen, lotions, or oils

Inform your provider of recent sun exposure or any medical changes

MODERATE TREATMENT (Fractional CO₂ / Moderate Downtime)

[Post Treatment Care](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/)

4 Weeks Before Treatment

Stop exfoliating acids, scrubs, and harsh active products

Avoid chemical peels, microneedling, or laser treatments

Notify your provider of any new medications

If you have a history of cold sores, antiviral medication may be prescribed — take as directed

Avoid NSAIDs (ibuprofen, aspirin, naproxen) unless prescribed by your physician

Avoid alcohol and smoking/vaping, as they may impair healing

Notify your provider if you are taking antibiotics or have any active infection

Do not apply makeup, lotions, sunscreen, or active skincare products

Ensure skin is healthy and intact (no rashes, infections, or open wounds)

Inform your provider of any recent sun exposure or medical changes

AGGRESSIVE TREATMENT (Ablative CO₂ / Full Resurfacing)

4–6 Weeks Before Treatment

Strict sun avoidance; no tanning beds or self-tanners

Discontinue all retinoids and active skincare

Discontinue exfoliating acids, scrubs, and harsh skincare

Avoid chemical peels, microneedling, or other laser treatments

Notify your provider of all medications and supplements

Antiviral prophylaxis may be prescribed if you have a history of cold sores — take as directed

Avoid NSAIDs unless prescribed by your physician

Avoid alcohol and smoking/vaping

Notify your provider if you are taking antibiotics or have any active infection, as this may require postponement or modified post-procedure care

Do not apply makeup, lotions, sunscreen, or active skincare products – Skin must be healthy and intact (no rashes, infections, open wounds, or active acne)

Inform your provider of: – Recent sun exposure – Pregnancy or breastfeeding – Active acne, cold sores, or skin infections – Any changes in medical history or medications

Important Notes

CO₂ laser treatment cannot be performed on tanned or sunburned skin – Treatment may be postponed if the skin is irritated, compromised, or infected – Downtime and results vary based on treatment depth and individual healing response

CO2 Laser Post-Treatment Instructions

What to Expect

Warm or sunburn-like sensation for up to 24 hours

Mild redness and possible light swelling

Skin may feel rough or dry for 3–7 days

Minimal downtime; most patients resume normal activities quickly

Post-Care Instructions

Apply a thin layer of Alastin Nectar then Alastin Soothe & Protect and/or ZOHydrating Cream regularly — do not let skin dry out

Avoid touching or cleansing the area for the first 24 hours

[Pre Post](https://benpepsi.com/blog/6290593955.html)

After 24 hours, cleanse gently with a mild cleanser

Avoid sun exposure, intense workouts, and sweating– If sweating occurs, cleanse gently with a 50/50 vinegar and water mix

Sleep with a clean pillowcase and head slightly elevated

Avoid makeup and makeup brushes for 48 hours– Mineral makeup is preferred if needed

A cool misting spray may be used for comfort

Downtime

Redness similar to a sunburn for 2–3 days

Light peeling or rough skin may occur; gentle exfoliation is ok after day 4

MODERATE TREATMENT

Moderate redness and swelling

Peeling and dryness typically begins 2–7 days post-treatment

[Post Treatment Care](https://benpepsi.com/blog/4413068746.html)

Possible clear drainage or light oozing in first 24–48 hours

Downtime varies but typically 5–7 days

Post-Care Instructions (First 48–72 Hours)

Do not shower face or neck for 48 hours

Wash hands thoroughly before touching treated areas

Perform vinegar soaks (see at bottom) every 4–6 hours

[Pre Post Treatment](https://metabolic-horizon.bbsxyxy.com/blog/2753877074.html)

After each soak:– Pat dry (do not rub)– Apply a thick layer of Alastin Soothe & Protect

After 24 hours:– Use Alastin Nectar during the day– Continue Soothe & Protect at night

Keep head elevated while sleeping

Avoid heat, sun exposure, and sweating

After First Week

Continue Alastin Nectar twice daily for 2-4 weeks

Begin prescription-grade SPF once skin has healed

Do not pick or pull peeling skin

AGGRESSIVE TREATMENT

Significant redness and swelling (may last 1–2 weeks)

[Post Treatment](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

Exudates/bleeding/crusting. Clear fluid (exudates) or blood may ooze onto skin. Frequent wound cleaning and Soothe and Protect during the first days post procedure is critical in preventing crusting. Do not rub/scrub.

Acne or milia formation: Flare-up of acne or formation of milia possible and may appear 2-4 days post treatment. This is usually self-limiting and resolves within one week. Do not pick at lesions.

[Pre Post Treatment](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/5930858196.html)

Risk of infection from bacterial, viral or fungal agents exists despite our bestpreventative effort. Wash your hands! Keep your environment clean! This is a must.

Scarring may occur from crusting, infection, or simply being exposed to laser. This risk is extremely low with the Fractionated CO2 laser.

Itching usually occurs as a part of the normal wound healing process. This usually occurs 3-4 days post procedure and may last up to one week. Cold compresses (20 minutes on and off) as needed to alter sensation can help. An oral antihistamine such as Zyrtec or Benadryl can also help.

Redness may persist for several weeks to months

Downtime typically 7–14+ days

Post-Care Instructions (Critical First Week)

Vinegar soaks:– Every 2–3 hours for first 24 hours– Every 3–4 hours after 24 hours– Continue daily or as needed after 48 hours

Apply Alastin Nectar then Soothe & Protect after every cleansing

Do not rub, scrub, or pick at skin

Keep environment clean; infection prevention is critical

Head elevation and strict sun avoidance required

Take prescribed medications as directed

Tylenol for discomfort; antihistamines may be used for itching

When starting to use make-up again, please make sure brushes/tools are clean

Long-Term Care

Strict sun protection

Notify your provider if skin appears unusually dark or light
