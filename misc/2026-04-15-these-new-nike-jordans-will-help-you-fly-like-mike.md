# These New Nike Jordans Will Help You Fly Like Mike

[Will Help You Fly](https://leanorbit.dns0663.com/blog/5501076615.html)

There’s always been a mystique about Air Jordan sneakers: Once a pair of kicks has that iconic flying Jordan logo on it, it’s supposed to deliver something special.

The new Jordan Super.Fly 2017 is the second pair of kicks we’ve seen this year that’s equipped with Nike’s new React sole, but the first pair that aims to combine off-court style and a premium feel with off-court usefulness.

And this shoe’s got hops, even if it does have flaws. Like the 2017 Hyperdunks, the Super.Fly delivers a ridiculously comfortable and soft ride for your heel and midfoot, with plenty of cushioning on landings and cuts. A tough, rigid construction secures your ankle nicely, and the lacing system locks down your midfoot, allowing you to move with aggression all over the court. These kicks also feature impressive traction; even on a dusty court, I never lost my grip. Everything about these Jordans is safe, sturdy and powerful; if you’re a baller who spins a lot or drives the lane, you’ll love that you’re never slipping around.

Unlike the Hyperdunks, these kicks, like most Jordans, can hold their own in the style department, too. There’s an all-black version that plays it safe, and then a sail/infrared color configuration that skillfully blends look-at-me with sophistication. Both pairs are less like the Jordans of recent years and a little more throwback, as natural-looking in jeans as they are in hoops shorts.

[Will Help You](https://insulin-pathways.hbsnzj.com/blog/7963047046.html)

There’s a durability to the Super.Fly, too. Most sneakers show hints of wear around the toebox after just days of walking around, but not these kicks; the material is so strong that a pair I wore around and played in for three weeks in New York City has only the slightest creases. Combined with the longevity of the React sole, the Super.Fly is built to last for a good long time, longer than your average pair of basketball kicks. You’ll be flying up and down the court in these babies for months, even if you play every day.

But that quality and strong build come at a price: You’ll spend far longer than you want breaking these babies in. The upper on these Jordans is incredibly tight, as is the fit on the toes. For about a week, walking in them downright hurt my toes, which felt scrunched in. These kicks run true to size, but wide-footed fellas just may want to pass because of that super-tight fit.

Everyone else, however, should think about the Super.Fly because it’s a well-rounded shoe that’s relatively affordable ($140, Buy It Here). And you’ve gotta love any pair of kicks that you can wear out for a night on the town too.
