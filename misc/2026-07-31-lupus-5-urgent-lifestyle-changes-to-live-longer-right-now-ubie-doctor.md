What lifestyle changes help people live longer with lupus disease?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Living longer with lupus is possible by combining medical care with healthy lifestyle habits. Key strategies include eating a balanced, anti-inflammatory diet, exercising regularly, managing stress, prioritizing sleep, avoiding sun exposure, and not smoking. Consistent follow-up with your doctor is also essential.

Because lupus symptoms often overlap with other conditions and can change over time, understanding what you're experiencing is the first step toward better management and a longer, healthier life. Explanation

Living with lupus can be challenging, but making some lifestyle changes can help improve quality of life and potentially increase longevity.

Healthy Eating: Eating a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains is important. This helps reduce inflammation and supports overall health.

Regular Exercise: Engaging in regular physical activity, such as walking or swimming, helps maintain a healthy weight and improves mood and energy levels.

Stress Management: Managing stress through activities like yoga, meditation, or deep breathing exercises can reduce lupus flare-ups and improve well-being.

Rest and Sleep: Getting enough rest and good quality sleep is crucial for managing fatigue and supporting the immune system.
