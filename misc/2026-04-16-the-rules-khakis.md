# The Rules: Khakis

The far edges of style are charted. There are clear rules for black-tie occasions, and no rules for manning the couch in your undies. But the middle ground is less well mapped. What to wear to the event that's neither the opera nor a SpongeBob marathon? Khakis.

Crisp and inexpensive, the preppy classics have become multipurpose pants. But their versatility has become a vice; we're hooked on khakis. They've become our default trou, the predictable compromise for any occasion south of a suit but north of jeans. Make no mistake, khakis are a key player in every guy's wardrobe. But don't be a drone. Add some wit to the way you wear them.
