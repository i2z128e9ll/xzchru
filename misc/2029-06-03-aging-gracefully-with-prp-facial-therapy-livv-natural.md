Aging and skin changes

Aging is a natural process that causes many changes in your life. There’s no specific age when people begin to experience these gradual shifts.

Your skin undergoes many changes as you get older. Genetics, lifestyle, diet, and habits influence the severity of the effects.

Wrinkles are the most visible signs of skin aging. They form when the skin loses flexibility. Sun exposure is another cause of this condition.

Facial movement lines become more prominent due to elasticity loss. These lines appear on the forehead, above the nose, temples, upper cheeks, and around the eyes and mouth. Lack of elasticity may also lead to more bruising.

Adults also experience dry skin when the body produces less sebum to keep it moist.

Age spots appear on body parts exposed to the sun, like the face, hands, and forearms. UV radiation is one of the leading causes of pre-cancer and skin cancer.

You can’t prevent these undesirable aging effects, but you can defer or reduce them.

Apart from minimizing sun exposure and keeping your skin healthy, consider incorporating aesthetic therapies like PRP facials. These treatments revitalize your skin and restore its youthfulness.

What is platelet-rich plasma (PRP) therapy?

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) is blood that has a high platelet content. The cells are rich in growth factors and active proteins that help the body heal.

PRP therapy is safe and natural. It uses your body’s own blood and stimulates the body’s ability to heal and rejuvenate.

The PRP facial procedure

During PRP therapy, the doctor draws your blood and places it into a centrifuge to separate the platelets from other components. The treatment is also referred to as vampire facial due to the blood-extraction process.

The doctor re-injects the blood with a high platelet concentration into the treated area. The procedure sounds time-consuming, but only takes about 30–60 minutes.

You can combine this procedure with Botox, dermal fillers, and laser treatments to improve your overall aesthetic goals.

Your body produces less collagen with age, resulting in visibly wrinkly skin. PRP facial treatments put platelet-rich plasma back into the skin, stimulating cell production. Increased collagen and elastin make your skin appear tighter, fuller, and smoother.

Reduces fine lines and wrinkles

Before the therapy

Preparing for a PRP facial includes the following steps.

Avoid unprotected and excessive sun exposure several weeks before your procedure. Also, refrain from using tanning booths.

Drink plenty of water in the days before your therapy. Stay hydrated and eat a meal beforehand. You may feel lightheaded because the procedure involves blood extraction.

Clean your face and don’t apply makeup or other cosmetics. Your skin should be free from dust, dirt, and chemicals.

Aftercare

Your doctor may apply a balm after the procedure to soothe any irritation or redness. You may also put on makeup to cover any temporary blemishes. Most patients feel well enough to drive home, but your doctor may recommend that you arrange a ride.

There may be post-procedure bruising, swelling, and redness, which clears in a few days. People with cold sores or herpes simplex may have an outbreak. Inform your doctor if you have such a condition.

You may resume your activities the next day. Avoid sun exposure during the recovery period. Protect your skin with sunscreen and proper clothing.

There may be redness, but resist the urge to apply more products. Don’t use alcohol-based products and exfoliants. Cleanse your skin once daily and moisturize when needed. Add a light foundation to camouflage the discoloration.

Avoid vigorous activities and exercise until your skin heals. Sweating may cause more irritation. You risk swelling and bruising from demanding physical pursuits. Do this for at least 72 hours after your procedure.

PRP facial: A holistic approach to skin health

While treatments like PRP facials improve your aging skin’s appearance, skin health doesn’t stop there. It’s vital to adopt a comprehensive approach to skincare.

Avoid sun exposure to help your skin age slowly and reduce the risk of health issues, like cancer. Make applying sunscreen and wearing clothes that cover exposed skin a habit.

Eat a healthy diet to provide the body with the nutrition to protect the skin. Sebum and collagen production are two examples.

Make lifestyle changes that enhance well-being. Avoid smoking and excess alcohol consumption, as the substances can harm your body.

Incorporate more physical activity and exercise into your daily routine. Besides the physical effects, you also feel younger.

Get enough sleep. Studies show chronic poor sleep quality affects natural aging and reduces skin protection.

Be vigilant and treat skin conditions early to ensure the effects last longer. Cosmetic treatments also boost your self-worth by making you look younger.
