# 19 of Michael Jordan’s Greatest Style Moments Through the Years

There’s a reason why Michael Jordan is considered the greatest of all time. The star athlete played in the NBA for 15 seasons (most of which were for the Chicago Bulls), had a short stint in baseball, and still had time to play plenty of golf. Even better? He did it all in style. Whether talking to reporters during press conferences, arriving at basketball practices, or just hanging out with friends on the golf course, Jordan’s looks have always been on point. From his signature berets and futuristic shades to his eye-catching hoop earrings, we rounded up some of number 23’s most iconic moments through the years. Scroll through and get inspired to upgrade your day-to-day attire.

March 1984

The superstar accepted the Eastman Award in 1984 in a sleek gray suit.

[Michael Jordan Greatest](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/3833720599.html)

April 1988

The basketball player signed autographs during a promotion event in Chicago wearing his signature beret and a printed windbreaker.

May 1989

Jordan opted for a casual ensemble when visiting Late Night with David Letterman. The timeless outfit consisted of pleated gray pants, a bright blue suit jacket, and a crisp white tee. His silver jewelry and watch completed the look.

September 1991

He posed with the Saturday Night Live cast in 1991 rocking an all-blue tracksuit with a red baseball cap.

July 1992

Jordan looks cooler than ever for a round of golf. He's pictured here wearing a pair of striped blue and white linen shorts with a matching tee.

July 1993

[Jordan Greatest](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/)

Jordan wore an Upper Deck baseball cap and oversized reflective sunglasses at the MLB All-Star Game in 1993.

October 1993

Jordan sported denim on denim when throwing out the first pitch for a Major League Baseball game.

March 1995

[Through the Years](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/blog/3237297611.html)

No one does it better than Jordan. Here, he’s leaving basketball practice dressed in a studded leather jacket, black dress pants, and white sneakers.

Jordan donned an eye-catching pre-practice look in the mid-'90s consisting of a red, white, and blue tracksuit and sleek shades.

January 1996

In 1996, the star signed autographs for fans in an ankle-length khaki trench coat layered over an all-white look. The black Oakley hat seals the deal.

October 1996

That’s all, folks! He posed with Bugs Bunny in a buttoned dark gray suit with a notched neckline. The duo were at the Warner Bros. Studio store in New York City.

December 1998

Jordan is the king of the accessory game. In 1998, he rocked a blue and gray cap, metal framed shades, and his signature gold hoop earring for a golf tournament.

March 1999

He spoke to reporters a year later wearing a flipped-up bucket hat, a white ribbed turtleneck, and gold hoops.

February 2000

Jordan couldn't stop smiling at the ESPY Awards with his family in a vibrant gray suit.

April 2000

How does one person own so many cool suits? Jordan posed with an award at the 2000 Essence Awards in a next-level black-and-white look.

June 2005

[the Years](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/blog/5962349545.html)

The star dressed to the nines when walking red carpets, too. He attended the BET Awards in a reddish-brown pinstripe suit with matching tinted glasses.

May 2006

Jordan sported another rendition of his signature sunglasses-suit combo when he posed for a photo at the Kentucky Derby. Here, he wears a white and light gray striped ensemble with silver shades.

February 2006

He rocked a black turtleneck at an NBA Gala in 2006.

[Jordan Greatest Style](https://allpepay.com/blog/4464663012.html)

May 2007

Jordan donned a tucked-in white shirt, gray dress pants, and a black leather belt on the golf course. The outfit’s highlight may just be the crocheted-esque baseball cap. Now that's a standout finishing touch!
