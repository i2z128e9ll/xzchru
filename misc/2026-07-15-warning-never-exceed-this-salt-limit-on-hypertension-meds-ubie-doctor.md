How much salt should I limit if I'm on hypertension medicine?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

High blood pressure medications work best when paired with a low-sodium diet. If you take blood pressure medication, experts recommend limiting daily sodium intake to less than 2,300 milligrams, with an ideal target of 1,500 milligrams for optimal results. Reducing salt helps your medication work more effectively, lowers cardiovascular risk, and can improve overall blood pressure control.
