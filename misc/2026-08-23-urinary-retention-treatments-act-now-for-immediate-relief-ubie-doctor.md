Urinary retention happens when someone cannot empty their bladder completely. If symptoms are severe, it may become necessary to treat the urinary retention with a catheter. A catheter is a thin tube that is inserted into the bladder to help drain urine. This can bring quick relief and prevent damage to the bladder.

In some cases, medications might be used to help relax the muscles around the bladder and improve urine flow. These medications can be especially helpful if the urinary retention is caused by an enlarged prostate or other similar issues.

Sometimes, if medications and catheter use are not enough, surgery might be needed. Surgery can help fix any blockages or other problems that might be causing urinary retention.
