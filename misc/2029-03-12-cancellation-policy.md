Cancellation – 24 hour advance notice

Applies to only confirmed appointments.

We require at least 24 hour advance notice of cancellation to avoid cancellation fees. However if your appointment is confirmed a fee will still apply.

Contacting USA Providers: To provide cancellation or rescheduling notice, you must contact us: 1) reply to the appointment confirmation email directly, 2) call to your local provider , or 3) contact us via contact form.

Contacting International Providers: To provide cancellation or rescheduling notice, you must contact us: 1) reply to the appointment confirmation email directly or 2) contact us via contact form.

If you or a portion of your group cancels within the 24-hour advance period, you will receive a refund. However, individual appointments will also incur a $50 cancellation fee, and for group appointments, there will be a $50 cancellation fee per person.

Cancellation – within 2 hour advance notice and missed appointments

Missed appointments or cancellation notifications within 2 hours prior to appointment time are non-refundable.

If part of your group is not present for the appointment:

a) If your group consists of 3 individuals or fewer, any missing individual will be charged in full. b) If your group is composed of 4 individuals or more, any missing individual will be refunded for their services but will incur a $50 no-show fee per person. For example, if 10 people book, and 5 people show up, the 5 attendees will be charged in full, and the no-show 5 individuals will be refunded for their services but charged a $50 per person cancellation fee.
