What is Femoral Neck Fracture?

A break in the proximal femur at the level of the hip. This fracture is associated with a high risk of complications

Typical Symptoms of Femoral Neck Fracture

Hip pain

Increased tendency to trip and fall

Difficulty walking normally

Lower back pain that makes bending forward while standing difficult

Hip joint hurts when sleeping

Diagnostic Questions for Femoral Neck Fracture

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are you experiencing hip pain?

Have you been tripping or falling more often?

Do you have difficulty walking normally?

Do you have lower back pain that makes bending forward while standing difficult?

Does your hip hurt when you sleep?

Treatment of Femoral Neck Fracture

Surgery is required to treat the break in the majority of fractures. In younger patients, this can be done with fixing the bone so it can heal. In older patients, replacing the hip joint with a partial or total replacement may be necessary.
