# MVP Level Up: How to Make the Hollow Hold Even Harder

Since the early days of my training, I’ve viewed the hollow hold as a core training fundamental. It’s an abdominal exercise that you absolutely have to learn and master, no matter your training level, because of how it trains your core. The hollow hold works your core as an integrated unit, simultaneously challenging abs, obliques, and lower back muscles to build a balanced, strong total core package.

And by “progress” we don’t just mean “add more weight.” Instead, we’re diving deep into the expansive library of moves and exercise variations that you’ve seen across the site and in my Eb and Swole series. Now, we’re organizing them into a progression that overloads your muscles and your body for gains in strength, athleticism, posture, and overall movement. If you’ve ever struggled to figure out how to evolve your exercise routine, this series solves that for you, showing you different exercises build upon each other.

First up is that hollow hold, which is more than a beginner core move. The hollow hold has its basis in gymnastics training, and the more you learn its nuances, the more you realize that it can indeed be your ticket to six-pack abs, and a strong overall core, too.

Why? Because a hollow hold trains your core in one of its most important functions: Bracing. Your abs are meant to do a series of things, rotating your torso, battling anti-rotation, flexing your spine, and bracing against all rotation. Master the hollow hold, and you’ve mastered bracing. And once you’ve mastered that, you can learn to rotate your torso correctly, and to battle rotation, too.

You’ll learn to do all that (and a few other things you might not be expecting) in this Level-Up video. I’ll walk you through an entire Hollow Hold progression that you can use in a variety of ways. You can find your “level,” spend a few weeks mastering and owning it, then move to the next progression. Or you can jam 1 to 2 sets of every single exercise into a single workout, creating a ramping core session that’ll have your abs on fire.
