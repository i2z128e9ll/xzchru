What is Ischemic Colitis?

This is a severe condition where the blood supply to the large intestine is significantly reduced, causing inflammation and damage to the large intestine.

Treatment of Ischemic Colitis

This typically requires hospital care. Injectable antibiotics might be given to prevent infections, and medication might be given to treat any underlying cause. In severe cases, surgery may be needed as well.
