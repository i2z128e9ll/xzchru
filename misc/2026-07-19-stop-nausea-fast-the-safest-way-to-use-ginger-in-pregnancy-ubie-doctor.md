How to Use Ginger:

Forms of Ginger: You can use ginger in different forms such as ginger tea, ginger capsules, or fresh ginger.

Ginger Tea: Make ginger tea by steeping fresh ginger slices in hot water.

Ginger Capsules: These are available in health stores and pharmacies.

Fresh Ginger: You can chew small pieces or add them to food.

Recommended Dosage: It is generally suggested to use ginger in moderation. The studies indicate that small amounts, often around 1 gram per day, can be effective.

Safety: Studies suggest that ginger is safe for most pregnant women when used in recommended amounts.
