Understanding Chronic Inflammation

Inflammation is the body’s natural defense mechanism against injury or infection. Acute inflammation is short-lived and beneficial, helping the body heal. Chronic inflammation, however, is a persistent, low-grade immune response that can damage tissues and organs. [1]

Common causes include:

Chronic stress and poor stress response

Diets high in processed foods and sugar

Sedentary lifestyle and lack of exercise

Environmental toxins or pollutants

Underlying chronic illness

This ongoing immune system activation contributes to conditions like cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and autoimmune disorders.

How Inflammation Impacts Hormones

Chronic inflammation interferes with the body’s delicate hormonal communication system:

Cortisol dysregulation: Prolonged stress and inflammation elevate cortisol, eventually leading to adrenal fatigue and impaired stress response. [3]

Thyroid disruption: Inflammation can impair thyroid hormone production, causing fatigue, weight changes, and metabolic slowdown.

Lifestyle Tips to Reduce Chronic Inflammation

While medical guidance is essential, lifestyle changes can significantly reduce inflammation and support hormone balance:

Regular exercise: Movement reduces inflammation and boosts energy.
