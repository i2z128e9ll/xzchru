What to Consider

Material

Look for quick-drying, lightweight and breathable fabrics like polyester, nylon or a spandex-elastane blend.

Polyester

Known for its durability and fast-drying properties, polyester is highly resistant to wear and tear, making it a long-lasting fabric that maintains its shape and appearance over time. Also, the material is stain- and wrinkle-resistant. It’s a common choice for hybrid shorts due to its versatility and comfort.

Nylon

This material is strong and abrasion-resistant. It offers excellent durability and is often used in outdoor and athletic wear.

Spandex/Elastane

Often blended with other materials, spandex provides stretch and flexibility, enhancing comfort and range of motion. A small percentage (usually 5-10%) can make a huge difference in comfort and fit.

Fit

You should ensure the shorts fit well around the waist and thighs. Most styles come with elastic waistbands or adjustable drawstrings for a customizable fit.

Length

Choose a length that suits your preference and activities. Mid-thigh to just above the knee is common, but personal comfort is key.

5-inch inseam

This is the shortest length, which is ideal for athletes, beachgoers and fashion-forward individuals. It provides maximum freedom of movement, offers a modern, sporty look and makes your legs appear longer, enhancing height.

7-inch inseam

Casual style seekers and fitness enthusiasts should opt for this length for both friendly hangouts and light sports activities. It’s that happy medium between coverage and mobility, making it great for daily wear.

9-inch inseam

If you prefer to not show too much thigh and want a longer length, here is your best bet. Taller gents and outdoorsmen would appreciate it, especially for fishing, hiking and water sports.

Pockets

Check the pocket design. Mesh-lined pockets are ideal for drainage if you plan to go swimming. This feature makes hybrid shorts practical for carrying personal items like keys, phone and wallet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Are Hybrid Shorts?

Hybrid shorts for men are a versatile type of clothing that combines features of both casual shorts and swim trunks. They are designed to be functional for a variety of activities, making them suitable to be worn in and out the water.

What Materials Are Hybrid Shorts Made From?

Hybrid shorts are typically made from quick-drying materials like polyester, nylon or blends of these fabrics with spandex and elastane. These fabrics help you to transition seamlessly between land and water without missing a beat.

What Activities Are Hybrid Shorts Suitable For?

Hybrid shorts are ideal for activities such as swimming, hiking, volleyball, beach outings, water sports, casual wear and general outdoor adventures.
