What to Look for in an Arousal Gel

There aren’t any hard-and-fast rules about which gel you put on your softest spots. Still, there are a few things sexperts want you to keep in mind before adding them to your online shopping cards or genitals.

1. Ingredients

There are a number of different ingredients that might be in an arousal gel that enable it to make your parts pulse. “My personal rule of thumb is if you wouldn’t ingest it, you shouldn’t use it on your most sensitive and absorbent parts,” says Reeves. That’s why she recommends steering clear of ingredients that you’re allergic to, as well as fragrances and other additives.

2. Base

Categorically, they may be known as arousal gels. But actually, some have a consistency that is more similar to that of oil, balm, or lubricant. The main determinant of the texture of the product is its base. Typically oil-based products are looser and, well, more oily than other products. Meanwhile, products with silicone, aloe vera, or shea butter bases tend to be thicker. You should keep your desired texture and consistency in mind when picking a product. The main, base ingredients of the arousal gel will also dictate whether or not the gel is safe to be used on sex toys and/or with latex condoms. Oil-based products of any kind cannot be used with latex, as oil ruins latex durability. Meanwhile, silicone-containing gels or lubricants can’t be used with silicone sex toys, as silicone sticks to itself and thus will alter the shape of the toy.

3. Intended use

Most arousal gels are intended to be used externally on body parts like the clitoris or nipples. If you are hoping to use the product for vaginal intercourse or anal penetration, be sure that the toy is safe for internal use, suggests Reeves. Using a product internally that is not designed for such, puts you at undue risk for irritation, itchiness, and even infection. “While most arousal gels are also safe to apply to a penis, there are also arousal formulas designed specifically for people with penises, which may be the better option for people with that anatomy,” she says.
