What is Laboratoires Vivacy?

Laboratoires Vivacy is a French pharmaceutical company specializing in the development and manufacture of injectable medical devices for aesthetic and anti-aging medicine. They cater to both aesthetic and ophthalmologic medical fields, with their aesthetic portfolio being the most well-known globally. Known for its emphasis on scientific rigor, innovation, and product safety, Laboratoires Vivacy has built a strong global reputation among aesthetic professionals.

The brand blends biotechnology and dermatological science to deliver premium aesthetic solutions for facial rejuvenation and volume restoration.

When Was Laboratoires Vivacy Founded?

Laboratoires Vivacy was founded in 2007 in Archamps, near Geneva, France. Since then, the company has expanded into over 80 countries and remains a key player in the dermal filler market through continuous innovation and practitioner support. They are particularly recognized for combining R&D expertise with cutting-edge technology to develop safe, effective, and versatile dermal fillers and mesotherapy solutions.

What Kind of Product Is Stylage?

Stylage is Vivacy’s flagship line of hyaluronic acid (HA) dermal fillers. Designed for precision, natural results, and patient safety, Stylage products are well-regarded in aesthetic clinics for their flexibility and advanced rheology.

Unlike traditional fillers, Stylage includes mannitol to reduce oxidative stress and limit HA degradation post-injection. This helps minimize side effects such as swelling and extends the life of the filler.
