How to Choose Wedding Suits for Men

Fit: The most crucial aspect of a wedding suit is how it fits you: the best wedding suit should be impeccably tailored to your body shape and type. A well-fitted suit will make your whole vibe better and help you exude confidence. Consider getting a bespoke suit from Indochino or investing in professional tailoring for off-the-rack options.

Fabric: The best wedding suits will be made in materials that are comfortable and suitable for the season and climate of your wedding. Wool and wool blends are versatile options for all seasons and weather, with lightweight fabrics like linen, or cotton, or seersucker being great for warm-weather weddings. If your wedding day involves long hours, ensure the suit allows you to move freely and comfortably. Breathable fabrics and a well-ventilated design can help keep you cool.

Style and Details: Make sure your suit style fits your personal taste and the dress code of your wedding. Two-piece suits, three-piece suits, and tuxedos are the best options to choose from. Also pay attention to the suit's detailing, such as lapels (notch, peak, or shawl lapels), buttons (two or three or just one), and pockets. Consider a suit with a contrasting lapel or unique button detailing for added style.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Should a Men's Wedding Suit Fit?We're glad you asked. Just like any other suit, your wedding suit should fit impeccably. Whether you want to go baggy (i.e. trendy) or super slim, a suit's jacket should sit directly above the shoulder, and its sleeves should slightly be above the sleeves to show off your cuff links.

With your suit pants, you could go full Thom Browne and cut it above your ankles, but for most guys, we recommend ending it just above your dress shoe, punctuating with a deep crease.

What Is the Best Color Suit for a Wedding?Choose a suit color that complements your skin tone and aligns with your wedding's theme. Classic choices include black, navy, charcoal gray, and medium gray. Lighter shades like beige or light gray work well for daytime or summer weddings.

If you want to experiment, burgundy suits will add a rich and distinctive color to your wedding attire and are great for fall and winter weddings. Light blue suits have become more popular for spring and summer weddings since they look fresh and are great for casual or garden ceremonies.

How Much Should a Men's Wedding Suit Cost?Off-the-rack wedding suits are pre-made, come in standard sizes, and are generally more budget-friendly (from $200 to $800 or more). For these suits, you’ll also want to budget for alterations to ensure a proper fit. Custom-made or bespoke suits are tailored to your specific measurements, and the price for these can go over $1,000 easily, but brands like Indochino have made it easy to customize at under $800. If you opt for a designer wedding suit, the price can be high, but brands like Todd Snyder, BOSS, and Ted Baker have great wedding suits that won’t cost over $1,500.
