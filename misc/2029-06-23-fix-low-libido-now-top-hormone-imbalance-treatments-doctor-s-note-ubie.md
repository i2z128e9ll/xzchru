Explanation

When low libido is caused by hormone problems, one way to help is to bring hormone levels back to normal. For men, low testosterone is often the reason, and testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) can help boost sex drive. Hormone replacement works by giving your body the hormones it is missing so that you feel more energetic and interested in sex. In addition to hormone changes, other things can help. Improving sleep, eating healthy foods, and getting regular exercise all help your body work better and may raise your mood too, which can make you feel more interested in being intimate.
