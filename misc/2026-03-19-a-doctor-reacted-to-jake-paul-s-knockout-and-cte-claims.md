YouTuber-turned-boxer Jake Paul faced off against former UFC fighter Ben Askren last night, and knocked out his opponent in the first round. The whole fight lasted just under two minutes. In a new video, YouTuber and physical rehab expert Dr. Brian Sutterer breaks down exactly what happened in that KO moment.

He explains that concussion occurs as a consequence of rapid acceleration and deceleration of the head, which causes the brain to impact against the inside of the skull. "Whenever that trauma occurs to the brain, you get a massive depolarization, or release of electric charge, that causes this massive chain of events in the brain that result in you losing consciousness, developing the symptoms of a concussion," he says.
