ALO YOGA IS collaborating with another decorated athlete—one who has, quite literally, pretty big shoes to fill.

Jimmy Butler, the 6-foot-7, six-time NBA All-Star is launching his own limited edition take on Alo's Recovery Mode Sneaker, dropping on October 1. You'll be able to grab his shoes at Alo's website and its three flagship stores in Beverly Hills, Calif.; Valley Fair, Calif; and Miami.

In a press release, the Golden State Warriors’ swingman calls the cushy shoe—reminiscent of Nike's iconic Air Force 1—a staple in his off-day footwear rotation. Butler’s version comes in a crisp all-white upper with a beige outsole that's clean enough to pair with any off-duty fit. No wonder it’s his go-to sneaker for post-game comfort, whether he’s “making coffee or playing dominoes." “I’m close with the Alo team and have been wearing the Recovery Mode for a while now,” he says.

Butler is widely regarded as one of the most intense, hardworking players in the NBA. Selected by the Chicago Bulls with the 30th pick of the first round draft in 2011, the five-time All-Defensive Team honoree averaged just 2.6 points per game as a rookie before developing into one of the league's best two-way players. Known as “Jimmy Buckets,” he was named the NBA’s Most Improved Player in 2015 and led two Miami Heat teams, including the eighth-seeded 2023 play-in club, to the Finals.

“Jimmy’s approach to both style and performance mirrors so much of what we believe at Alo,” says Summer Nacewicz, EVP of Creative and Marketing, citing that the partnership brings a modern upgrade to the Recovery Mode sneaker.

Like Butler, Alo’s footwear line has evolved. The Recovery Mode shoe made its debut in 2023 as the brand's bridge between training gear and streetwear. For $195, the sneaker packs a cushy foam insole and padded collar that you can comfortably wear for hours. Beyond its clean look, the Warriors star’s sneaker adds an extra-tough rubber outsole that might help with balance.
